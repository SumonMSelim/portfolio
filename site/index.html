01 / WHOAMI

Kept systems up. Now learning to keep them safe.

I'm a Senior Software Engineer based in the Netherlands. For over 10 years I've been building software systems that need to stay reliable at scale, working mostly on backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, platform modernisation and DevOps automation.

A big part of my work is turning complex systems into simpler, more dependable ones. I have led work on distributed platforms, multi-region infrastructure, CI/CD migrations and internal engineering standards.

After a decade of keeping systems up, I wanted to understand how to keep them safe. That's why I'm doing an MSc in Security and Networking Engineering at the University of Amsterdam. This semester has been mostly Cryptography and Digital Forensics.

Outside work I mentor, speak and organise: AWS Community Builder, HashiCorp User Group organiser, and founder of Klassroom.xyz, a non-profit that helps young people build practical tech skills.
